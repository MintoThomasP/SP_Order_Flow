1. Executive Workflow Overview

This Standard Operating Procedure (SOP) defines the operational, technical, and quality frameworks governing all Print-on-Demand (POD) orders received by Silver Point Printing & Digital Solutions L.L.C. from Peecho B.V. (Amsterdam, Netherlands). Fulfilling orders across the Gulf Cooperation Council (GCC), Middle East, and Indian subcontinent, Silver Point combines cloud-native REST automation with high-precision digital press manufacturing.

✓ Core Manufacturing Principle: Barcode-Verified Custody & Zero-Defect Production
Every individual photobook, magazine, and custom book printed for Peecho is assigned a unique cryptographic payload hash, an internal tracker (SP-2026-XXXXX), and an encrypted 2D QR Job Ticket. Workstation operators scan items at every machine interface, providing Peecho with automated, auditable milestone sync from webhook ingestion to carrier waybill handoff.

2. End-to-End Workflow Step-by-Step

STEP 1 Inbound Webhook Order Reception & Ingest Validation
Latency: < 400 ms

Orders submitted through Peecho's e-commerce widgets or client APIs hit Silver Point's hardened webhook endpoint: POST /api/v1/peecho/webhook/.

HMAC-SHA256 Security
The raw HTTP body is cryptographically checked against header X-Peecho-Signature using the shared integration secret. Unauthorized requests are immediately blocked with HTTP 401.
Deduplication Guard
A SHA-256 payload hash is compared against PeechoPayloadLog. Identical payload submissions return HTTP 200 with status DUPLICATE, preventing accidental double production.
Dynamic SLA Timer Engine
The priority tier is parsed to initialize a countdown clock: RUSH_8H (8 Hours), EXPRESS_24H (24 Hours), or STANDARD_72H (48–72 Hours).
Synchronous ACK Return
Silver Point responds with HTTP 201 Created and the internal tracking identifier (SP-2026-XXXXX) within < 400 milliseconds (guaranteed SLA < 2.0s).
STEP 2 S3 PDF Download & Prepress Artwork Verification
Automated Preflight

An asynchronous Celery task streams high-resolution print files (pdf_artwork_url and pdf_cover_url) directly to the encrypted S3 bucket silverpoint-pod-artwork-vault without overloading application memory.

Resolution & Color Check
Automated preflight checks that raster graphics meet or exceed 300 DPI at full scale. Color spaces are verified for CMYK (Fogra 39 / Fogra 51). RGB files trigger an automated color conversion audit.
Bleed & Safety Margins
Verifies that a mandatory 3.0 mm bleed box exists on all trim sides. Text safe margin requires 5.0 mm clearance from the cut edge to prevent content loss during high-speed cutting.
Spine Caliper Formula
Dynamically computes exact spine thickness: Spine (mm) = (Page Count / 2) * Caliper + Board (Hardcover board: 3.0mm; Softcover board: 0.8mm; 200gsm Silk = 0.200mm/leaf).
Prepress Operator Gate
Prepress technicians conduct a visual overlay inspection in the Next.js Workspace. Approving advances the order to PREPRESS_PASSED; anomalies trigger EXCEPTION_HOLD.
STEP 3 Production Imposition Batching & QR Job Ticket Generation
Press Batching

Orders approved for print are grouped into optimized production batches (ProductionBatch) to maximize sheet yields on our HP Indigo 12000 (B2) and Canon press lines while minimizing substrate changeovers.

Clustering Parameters
Orders are clustered by Substrate Weight (e.g. 200gsm Silk Inner + 350gsm Gloss Cover), Trim Dimensions (A4, A5, Square 210x210mm), and Binding Format.
Printable A4 QR Job Ticket
A machine-readable PDF Job Ticket is generated using ReportLab, embedding a 2D QR code (SP-POD-BATCH:<id>|PRESS:<machine>) and 1D Code 128 barcodes for each order item.
STEP 4 Shopfloor Execution Tracking via Barcode & Mobile PWA Scanning
Floor Execution

Operators at each physical machine station update order milestones using wireless industrial 2D scanners or mobile PWA cameras running the Silver Point Floor Scanner:

STEP 5 Barcode Dispatch Station & Waybill S3 Archiving
Dispatch Gateway

At the packing bench, items are sealed in moisture-barrier wrap with protective corner guards. The operator scans the order barcode into the Dispatch Terminal:

Automated Carrier Routing
The system evaluates destination and SLA to select the optimal tier-1 carrier: DHL Express (Europe, Americas, Global), Aramex (GCC domestic and cross-border), FedEx (International), or BlueDart (India).
Thermal Label & S3 Archiving
An Air Waybill (AWB) is requested from the carrier API, rendering a 4x6" thermal label. The PDF waybill document is archived in S3 at s3://silverpoint-pod-artwork-vault/awb/.
STEP 6 Automated Reverse Tracking API Callbacks to Peecho
Sync < 5.0s

Within < 5.0 seconds of waybill generation, an automated Celery background worker executes a reverse REST callback to Peecho:

POST https://api.peecho.com/v2/orders/{peecho_order_id}/shipment

Payload includes tracking_code, carrier, tracking_url, awb_url, and shipped_at. Retries utilize exponential backoff (30s, 60s, 120s, 240s, 480s) to guarantee delivery during upstream Peecho maintenance.

3. Quality Control & Re-Print Logic

Silver Point operates under a strict Zero-Defect Quality Policy. If an item fails physical inspection at the Quality Audit Table, the following remediation procedure executes immediately:

Defect Category Inspection Checkpoint & Tolerance Immediate Remediation Action
Print Surface Flaws Streaking, roller marks, banding, or color drift ($\Delta E > 2.0$). Item rejected. Densitometer recalibration on HP Indigo; auto reprint queued.
Cutting / Trim Errors Trim inaccuracy exceeding $\pm 0.5\text{ mm}$ or cut edge roughness. Item rejected. Polar Mohr blade inspected; job block re-printed on rush queue.
Bindery & Adhesion PUR spine crack, loose leaves on pull-test, or cover warp. Item rejected. Adhesive tank temperature checked; book block remanufactured.
Lamination Defects Bubbling, silvering, peeling, or scratches on cover wrap. Item rejected. Thermal laminator pressure adjusted; cover re-impressed.
⚠ Automated Instant Re-Print Requeuing Algorithm
When a QC inspector clicks "Log Scrap & Request Reprint" on the shopfloor scanner, the system instantly logs the scrap reason in the ERP register and spawns a high-priority reprint order (REPRINT_QUEUED). The reprint receives top priority on the HP Indigo press queue (RUSH_8H), ensuring the overall SLA deadline remains unbreached without requiring intervention from Peecho's team.
